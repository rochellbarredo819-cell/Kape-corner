About and help

Kape Corner is a school project that shows how an online ordering system works using only HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. All shop names, people, and orders are made up.

  1. Choose items from the menu and press Add.
  2. Change quantities in the cart (1 to 20 per item).
  3. Fill in your details and press Place order.
  4. Find your order under My orders. You can update its status, edit your details, cancel it, or delete it.

Your orders stay after refreshing the page, but only on this browser and device.
